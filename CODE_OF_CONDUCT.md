# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Treat people with respect, provide constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on technical evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply to project discussions, reviews, events and private interactions about the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn contributors, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and protect the privacy of everyone involved. Report concerns privately to a maintainer through the contact details on their repository profile. Maintainers must recuse themselves from concerns involving their own conduct.

This policy draws on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
